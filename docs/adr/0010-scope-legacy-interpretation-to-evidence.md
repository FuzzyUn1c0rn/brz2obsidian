# Keep legacy interpretation scoped to qualified evidence

Replay historical reviews under their recorded output generation. The recorded-6
grammar retains bounded identity replay without extending it to structured
merging or reproduction of the original output. Recorded-7 reviews keep their
qualified replay meaning, and new format-8 operations can qualify edited native
metadata while retaining the baseline generation for identity and merging.
When reproducing format-8 bytes, add the category role tag to the existing
membership set. Copying the set can change case-fold ties, and the historical
record lacks the producer's hash seed. A matching record alone cannot establish
exact original tag order.

Only frozen, previously qualified Local API captures may use the legacy
interpretation of a leading BOM. Verify the complete interpretation, including
findings and exact deletion evidence. Fresh acquisition uses the current
interpretation. This preserves sealed historical meaning while keeping live
admission governed by current source policy.

Unmarked checkpoint storage from [issue #272](https://github.com/FuzzyUn1c0rn/brz2obsidian/issues/272)
retains its original semantic fingerprint. Qualify the Undo Prepared and
recovery Prepared variants with their operation-specific fields so their
meaning is bound without changing the fingerprint of an older checkpoint.

See [ADR 0006](0006-review-output-format-upgrades-separately.md).
