# Keep legacy interpretation scoped to qualified evidence

Replay historical reviews under their recorded output generation. The recorded-6
grammar retains its bounded identity behavior. Recorded-7 reviews keep their
qualified replay meaning, and new format-8 operations can qualify edited native
metadata while retaining the baseline generation for identity and merging.
Format 8 added the category role to the already-built membership set. Copying
that set can change case-fold ties, and the historical record lacks the
producer's hash seed. A matching record alone cannot establish exact original
tag order.

Only frozen, previously qualified Local API captures may use the legacy
interpretation of a leading BOM. Verify the complete interpretation, including
findings and exact deletion evidence. Fresh acquisition uses the current
interpretation. This preserves sealed historical meaning while keeping live
admission governed by current source policy.

See [ADR 0006](0006-review-output-format-upgrades-separately.md).
