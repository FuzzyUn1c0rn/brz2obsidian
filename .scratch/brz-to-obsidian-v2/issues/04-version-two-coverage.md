- **PDF content search:** Q4 is accepted: preserve PDFs and explicitly accept the built-in vault Search limitation for v2. No PDF extraction/indexing capability is required by this decision. Q5 limits semantic adaptations to reviewed features; Q7 explains remote/offline limits without treating service availability as missing archived bytes.
- **Remaining inventory and compatibility:** Q6 carries forward ordinary Markdown and exact metadata/history retention, with system-to-type connections retained only. Q8 requires the two earlier archives to remain acceptance cases. The user clarifies that all supplied archives come from the same TheBrain version: converter releases progressively support more of its features, rather than tracking different TheBrain versions.

## Answer

### Cumulative feature coverage

Version 2 extends the converter's reviewed feature coverage for the same TheBrain version used by all three supplied archives. These archives are complementary acceptance cases, not separate application-version targets or a fingerprint allowlist. An archive can convert when it fits the reviewed feature classes and integrity rules. The shared format/application version alone does not establish that all its features are supported. Later converter versions may add feature classes when more complex archives expose them.

The [complete source comparison](../research/source-differences.md), [native observations](../research/native-source-behavior.md), and accepted [thought-type representation](03-thought-type-representation.md) establish the following disposition for every observed difference.

### Reporting and destination limits

Report every approved missing TXT declaration in the migration report and retain a brief source-filename notice on its existing owner note. Such a notice does not create a note solely for missing TXT, a fabricated file or a broken local link. Keep the existing successful-with-warnings CLI outcome/exit code 2; warn about the source gap honestly even though its PDF is intact. The new archive currently establishes four such source-gap records; final conversion must verify those findings against the agreed predicate.

Preserve PDFs without requiring PDF text extraction or indexing. Explain once in the migration report and usage guide that built-in Obsidian vault Search does not provide the evidenced TheBrain PDF body-search behavior. PDF preservation and native opening remain required; preservation alone does not claim search equivalence or establish fresh TheBrain restore behavior.

Explain remote video playback's network/service dependence once in that report and guide. A preserved URL remains available when an embed is blocked or offline; remote availability is not an archived-payload integrity check. Check native embed rendering and any timing behavior in the disposable trial, and record actual limitations rather than promising unverified equivalence. Accepted destination limitations are distinct from per-record source-gap warnings.

### Refusal and backward compatibility

Keep refusal for corrupt/unsafe archives, invalid identities or ownership, unresolved required references, unaccounted bytes, competing note contents, unsupported meanings, and unmatched missing notes/files/images/PDFs. A warning exception requires its reviewed evidence; additional uninterpreted metadata does not excuse an unsupported semantic feature. Subtype inheritance, custom link types, custom fields and other unexamined capabilities remain outside this release's reviewed coverage. Investigate them when a future archive or explicit scope change requires them.

Both earlier accepted Brains remain required acceptance cases. Preserve their existing thought-note selection, source identity/naming, hierarchy/jumps/tags, attachment and original-note bytes, local-reference adaptation, metadata/history and warnings. In particular, retain the previous positive-length absent-TXT rule and icon-length exception; do not retroactively impose the new zero-length case's stricter PDF identity association on the older rule. Their accepted two/nine warning counts and successful-with-warnings outcomes remain regression expectations. Generated version 2 support artifacts are counted separately from source thought notes and present payloads; an entire vault need not be byte-identical to version 1.

Final acceptance must independently verify all three complete archives, including original bytes/metadata, every supported relationship and reference, warning predicates and native usability. The accepted source-neutral architecture and one-time fresh-vault workflow carry forward. Detailed schemas, rendering mechanics and testing seams belong in the required to-spec artifact after template decisions and the disposable trial; this ticket accepts coverage without implementing it.
