    What is proposed, and what has been observed?

    The state and history above illustrate the accepted behavior from #240, #243 and #248. This page does not establish filesystem safety, semantic merge correctness, persistence, exact-byte storage or Git compatibility.

    Observed separately: the disposable experiment on Git 2.55.0.windows.5 preserved the real user index, staged entries, HEAD, branches, tag and configuration; round-tripped mixed line endings, UTF-16, ident and binary bytes; and used fresh processes to detect partial publication and preserve later edits. These bounded observations do not prove every transition shown here. The experiment report identifies its authoritative run, exact source hash, timing and limits.

    Mechanism evidence: see the separately retained experiment report for executed commands, real restart observations, inventories, storage/time measurements and limits. Any scenario absent from that report remains unverified.

    Compatibility and missing-history boundaries used here

    These are the accepted conditional policies, not successful test results. The full authoritative C01–C17 and H01–H09 matrices remain in #248.

    • Ordinary complete local vault-root repositories, including staged/unstaged/untracked work, are conditional targets. Converter history must preserve user HEAD, branches and index. Publication may add unstaged differences.
    • Built-in line-ending/encoding/ident transforms require a specific exact-byte snapshot choice. A changed effective policy needs review again. Excluded authored files have a separate inclusion choice if affected; unrelated excluded content may stay outside recovery with disclosure.
    • Initially refuse applicable custom filters, LFS, active hooks/drivers, shared/linked/nested/parent/bare layouts, sparse/index suppression, incomplete/external/unsupported object stores, unsafe paths/ownership/storage and unsupported Git. Existing operations and locks must be resolved first. No generic bypass or separate history store.
    • Missing converter history is not repaired by identities or an OID alone. Validate a matching complete history copy, or explicitly onboard while preserving current content and uncertainty. Older Undo may be unavailable. Resolve interruptions before onboarding.
    • A complete consistent copy must include actual converter refs/objects and relevant configuration. Sync or selective copies may omit history; no automatic remote fetch, push or history transport is promised. Independent branch rewriting alone does not prove converter history lost.
    • Keep required history until explicit cleanup review, retain the update baseline, and respect independent user history. Ordinary large binaries are conditional on measured storage/time limits.
